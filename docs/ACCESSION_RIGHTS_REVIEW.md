# Accession-specific rights review — bounded, not blanket clearance

Reviewed for local version 1.0.1. This review distinguishes a copyright license, a source-accession notice and patent/other-IP questions. It does not infer that the author owns source sequence content merely because it appears in a derived file.

## UniProt references

All eight frozen accession/version/hash records are enumerated in provenance/uniprot_accession_review.tsv. The UniProt Consortium's database-wide CC BY 4.0 copyright terms are retained, including in mixed FASTA and alignment records. Direct entry views for Q88VJ2, P26298, P26297, D2RJU7, P37666 and P36234 returned JavaScript fallbacks; Q5U922 direct view failed, while indexed official content for Q5U922 and P36234 and substantive P17584 content were available. These access differences do not remove the documented database copyright license, but they prevent a claim that every current accession-specific notice was fully inspected.

A concrete other-IP lead was found for **P37666**: patent publication [EP3280794B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3280794B1/en) includes that accession in its specification's enzyme list. A mention in a specification is not a finding that its claims cover this sequence archive, that a relevant claim is valid/in force, or that redistribution is prohibited. Current legal status, jurisdictions and claim applicability were not determined. No patent license or freedom-to-operate conclusion is asserted. Other references were not subjected to an exhaustive patent search.

## Genomic sources, including selected proteins outside the eight full proteomes

provenance/ncbi_accession_review.tsv covers all 507 metadata accessions, with separate flags for full proteomes, selected screen proteins and HMMER origins. The eight full-proteome assembly records were actually opened: GCF_000832905.1, GCF_014131795.1, GCF_000023085.1, GCF_000829055.1, GCF_046109915.1, GCF_000155515.2, GCF_900636965.1 and GCF_004771075.1. Four returned reCAPTCHA and four only a dynamic page shell; none supplied a complete accession-specific rights notice in the retrieved view. The other records were not individually inspected in this bounded review and are explicitly marked accordingly.

The [NCBI molecular-data policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) imposes no NCBI reuse/distribution restriction but does not transfer rights held or claimed by submitters. No specific prohibition was established here; **complete accession-specific redistribution clearance remains unverified**, rather than assumed. The author's approval covers his code/documentation/results, not those underlying third-party sequences.

## Consequence for publication

The owned-material licenses are finalized. Treat the included source sequences under their existing source terms/notices and resolve any applicable accession/submitter restriction before declaring the entire archive unrestricted or fully rights-cleared. No actual prohibition is invented from an inaccessible page or a patent citation. The public-reuse assessment for underlying third-party content remains open, and the notices do not convert it to MIT or the author's CC BY grant. This local archive is not a publication or submission and was not uploaded.
